Variational Surrogate Jacobians: A Framework for Constrained Gradient Propagation

Dogan, A. H., Arasli, D., Oksuz, K., Akbas, E., & Kalkan, S. · Submitted · 2027

Backpropagation through discrete and constrained optimization layers requires informative replacements for degenerate derivatives. We introduce the Variational Surrogate Jacobian (VSJ), which selects the surrogate Jacobian closest to a chosen prior under a positive-definite metric while preserving the local geometry of the active constraints to first order. Under strict constraint enforcement, the resulting optimization problem admits a unique closed-form solution. We further introduce adaptive constraint weighting based on primal slacks and dual multipliers, allowing the enforcement strength of each constraint to vary with the local optimization state. VSJ recovers several established surrogate and sensitivity-based backward rules, including identity-style propagation, as particular configurations, and also applies directly to loss-dependent updates generated by optimization-aware methods.
